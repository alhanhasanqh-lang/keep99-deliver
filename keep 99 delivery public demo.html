Frequently asked questions

Is Keep99 Delivery accepting orders now?

Not yet. This website is a prelaunch demonstration. Restaurants and real checkout will be activated after the platform is built and launch areas are announced.

How much does Keep99 charge restaurants?

Our proposed model is a 1% Keep99 commission on food sales. Payment processing, refunds, taxes and other applicable costs are separate and will be clearly disclosed in the final restaurant agreement.

How much do drivers earn?

Under the proposed model, drivers receive 99% of the customer delivery fee plus 100% of any optional customer tip. Actual earnings vary with deliveries, distance, time, costs and demand.

Are customer tips required?

No. Tips are always optional. If a customer leaves a tip, Keep99 does not retain any of it.

Are menu prices marked up?

No Keep99 markup. Our intended restaurant policy is to list the same prices as the restaurant’s regular direct menu. This must be verified when restaurants are onboarded.

What do customers pay beyond their food?

Only the disclosed delivery fee and an applicable, legally permitted card-processing charge, plus required taxes. Keep99 does not add a customer service fee. Tips are entirely optional and go 100% to the driver. Card-processing fee treatment is still being finalized and may differ by payment method.

Where will Keep99 launch?

The first launch market has not been officially announced. The plan is to start in a focused local delivery area and expand based on demand and operational readiness.

Can my restaurant stay on other delivery apps?

Yes, the proposed model does not require exclusivity. Final partner terms will be published before onboarding.

Is driver registration an approval to deliver?

No. This is an interest form only. Actual onboarding will require eligibility checks, required documents, agreements and platform approval.
